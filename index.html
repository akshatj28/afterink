Afterink in two minutes

Companies sign thousands of supplier contracts, then file them away. Deadlines pass, invoices overcharge and refunds go unclaimed. Afterink reads every signed contract with AI, turns it into dates and prices, and puts each one in front of the team that has to act.

This is a working demo on a sample company, Kestrel Mobility. Everything you click works, the AI runs live, and nothing is ever sent to a supplier.

  1. Legal teamFiles each signed contract. AI reads it, code checks it.
  2. Buying teamDecides every renewal before the last day to say no.
  3. Finance teamPays only what the contract allows.
  4. EveryoneAsks questions and gets the clause as proof.
